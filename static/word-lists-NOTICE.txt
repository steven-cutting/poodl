Poodl's word lists come from three places. The guess dictionary merges
words derived from two collections -- SCOWL, by Kevin Atkinson, and
12dicts, by Alan Beale -- with a list of five-letter words the maintainer
supplied from a version of the game built with Replit, which neither
provided nor recorded where those words came from. The answer list was
supplied the same way and trimmed by hand. One entry comes from none of
these -- the game's own name, "poodl", which the specification requires
both lists to carry. See docs/how-to/replace-the-word-lists.md for the
provenance, for what is not known about it, and for the obligations any
replacement has to satisfy.
