=== THE ATTACHED IMAGES ===

IMAGE 1 IS THE CANON. It is the accepted drawing of this dog: her exact colours, her exact
proportions, her head shape, her ear set and length, her marking placement, her coat
length, her line weight and her register. EVERY drawing you make must be unmistakably THE
SAME DOG as Image 1, drawn by the same hand on the same day, in the same style, at the same
scale of detail. Do not restyle her. Do not redesign her. Do not make her cuter, furrier,
younger or more detailed than Image 1. Match Image 1's outline quality exactly: a calm,
smooth, evenly weighted line, no scallops, no bumps, no sticker rim.

THE REMAINING IMAGES ARE PHOTOGRAPHS of the real dog, attached for this pose only. Take the
POSE and the ATTITUDE from them and nothing else: no colour, no light, no background, and
none of the collars, harnesses, tags, hats or props any of them shows.

=== WHAT TO DRAW ===

Her HEAD AND NECK ONLY, filling the frame, in EXACT SIDE VIEW facing the viewer's left,
cropped just below the throat. Centred, generous even margin. A head study for a model sheet.

THIS IS THE PANEL THAT PROVES THE HEAD IS A CIRCLE WITH A STUB. Draw the skull as a round
dome and the muzzle as a SHORT BLUNT stub on the front of it, no longer than one third of the
skull's diameter, with a soft rounded stop. NEVER a wedge, NEVER a long snout, NEVER a
tapering show-poodle head. The near ear hangs as one soft lobe from a LOW set at eye level
down to about the jawline, in the darkest colour. The cream band wraps the muzzle from behind
the nose back to under the eye. The one visible eye is round, open and level, amber with a
flat dark pupil. The nose is large and liver brown. Mouth closed, one short flat stroke.
