=== THE ATTACHED IMAGES ===

IMAGE 1 IS THE CANON. It is the accepted drawing of this dog: her exact colours, her exact
proportions, her head shape, her ear set and length, her marking placement, her coat
length, her line weight and her register. EVERY drawing you make must be unmistakably THE
SAME DOG as Image 1, drawn by the same hand on the same day, in the same style, at the same
scale of detail. Do not restyle her. Do not redesign her. Do not make her cuter, furrier,
younger or more detailed than Image 1. Match Image 1's outline quality exactly: a calm,
smooth, evenly weighted line, no scallops, no bumps, no sticker rim.

THE REMAINING IMAGES ARE PHOTOGRAPHS of the real dog, attached for this pose only. Take the
POSE and the ATTITUDE from them and nothing else: no colour, no light, no background, and
none of the collars, harnesses, tags, hats or props any of them shows.

=== WHAT TO DRAW ===

One dog, alone, standing square on all four feet, in EXACT SIDE VIEW, facing the
viewer's left, seen from her own eye level. The whole animal in frame: the head in true
profile, the whole topline, the whole tail, and all four legs with the far pair just visible
behind the near pair. Centred, generous even margin.

THE HEAD IN PROFILE IS THE HARDEST PART AND THE MOST OFTEN GOT WRONG. It is a CIRCLE WITH A
SHORT BLUNT STUB on the front. The muzzle projects no further than one third of the skull's
diameter, the stop is a soft curve rather than an angle, and the whole head reads as round.
It is NEVER a wedge, NEVER a long snout, NEVER an elegant tapering show-poodle head.

Standing, so the tail is carried up and curved over the back, its last third the paler
plume. The visible eye is round, open and level. Mouth closed, flat. Third of four.
