=== WHO SHE IS (read this first; it decides everything else) ===

"Biscuit" is a brown miniature poodle. She is the host of a spare, precise little
word game for adults. She is exacting, proprietorial and entirely unbothered.

She is NOT a cute cartoon mascot. Not kawaii, not chibi, not a plush toy, not a
sticker, not a children's-book character, not a greetings-card puppy. If the drawing
would look at home on a nursery wall it is WRONG. The single most common failure is
making her adorable; do not.

Concretely, that means:
- Her EYES ARE SMALL relative to her skull — real dog eyes, each about one seventh of
  the skull's width. NEVER big round anime or cartoon eyes. No eyelashes. No sparkle.
- Her expression is DEADPAN. No smile. No happy curve to the mouth. No raised brows.
- Her head is NOT oversized. She is an adolescent dog with near-adult proportions,
  not a puppy with a big head and short legs.

=== THE GENRE THIS DRAWING BELONGS TO ===

Draw her as a MID-CENTURY MODERN SCREEN-PRINTED POSTER ANIMAL: the flat, geometric,
reduced style of 1950s and 1960s silkscreen wildlife and zoo posters, where an animal is
built from a few clean flat shapes and one ink line, printed in four or five spot colours
with no shading of any kind. Calm, graphic, adult, slightly severe. The kind of print that
hangs in a design studio, not a nursery.

That genre decides the drawing more than any other instruction here:
- REDUCE. Draw the fewest shapes that still read as this dog. Simplify the coat into clean
  masses. Resist adding detail; detail is the enemy of this style.
- NO STICKER LOOK. This is a printed poster, not a die-cut vinyl sticker. There is NO white
  rim, halo, glow or outline running around the outside of the animal. The ink line is a
  drawn line of even width, not a cut edge.
- NO CUTENESS. Mid-century poster animals are observed, not anthropomorphised. She is
  poised and self-possessed, never adorable, never appealing to a child.

=== RENDERING LANGUAGE (a screen print, not a painting) ===

Draw her as a FLAT VECTOR ILLUSTRATION: one ink keyline plus flat spot colours, like
a two-plate screen print or a modern pictogram.

LINE
- ONE ink line, pure white (#ffffff), the SAME WIDTH EVERYWHERE — about 1/200 of the
  image width. No tapering, no pressure, no thicker outer contour, no double line,
  no sketchiness, no broken strokes, no halo, no glow, no drop shadow, no outer
  white rim offset from the fill.
- THE SILHOUETTE IS ONE SMOOTH CONTINUOUS CURVE with a slight, irregular, lazy waver
  in it. It is NOT scalloped. It is NOT a row of bumps. It is NOT a chain of arcs or
  semicircles. It is NOT a cloud outline. It is NOT drawn as individual curls. Think
  of the edge of a soft cushion drawn in a single confident stroke, not of a sheep.
- Coat texture inside the silhouette is a HANDFUL of separate open loops of the white
  line — about six to twelve in a whole-body drawing, and none at all over large areas
  of flank. Never a scribble, never a fur pattern, never hatching, never fur strands.

FILLS
- Flat fills ONLY. Every colour area is one completely even colour. NO shading, NO
  gradient, NO highlight, NO catchlight, NO cast shadow, NO ambient occlusion, NO
  texture, NO grain, NO noise, NO airbrush. Light does not fall on her.
- Exactly this palette and nothing else; no mixing, no tints, no darker or lighter
  versions of any of them:
    body (skull, back, flanks, rump, upper legs, tail shaft) : #8a5230
    ear hair (the darkest thing on her)                      : #6b3f22
    cream markings                                           : #efd3b4
    tail plume                                               : #dda97b
    nose leather                                             : #a9663b
    iris                                                     : #c4834e
    pupil, and paw pads                                      : #3d2313
    the ink line                                             : #ffffff
    the background                                           : #0b0b0b
- She is recognisably BROWN. The body is NEVER cream, beige, tan, blond, grey or
  café-au-lait. The photographs are faded, sunlit or filtered: take ANATOMY from them,
  NEVER colour.

BACKGROUND
- One flat #0b0b0b covering the whole image, edge to edge, fully opaque. No
  transparency. No floor, no ground line, no horizon, no shadow under her, no scene,
  no props, no vignette, no border, no frame, no sticker outline.

=== ANATOMY (each line names the mistake it prevents) ===

- SQUARE AND COMPACT. Height at the withers is about equal to the length from the
  point of the chest to the back of the rump. Not long-bodied. Not leggy. Not a
  sausage.
- THE HEAD IS A CIRCLE WITH A STUB. The skull is a round dome. The muzzle is a SHORT
  BLUNT stub on the front of it, no longer than one third of the skull's diameter,
  with a soft rounded stop rather than an angle. NEVER a long tapering show-poodle
  wedge, NEVER a snout, NEVER a greyhound head. This is the second most common
  failure after cuteness, and it is most often made in the profile view.
- THE NECK DISAPPEARS INTO COAT, so the head sits close to the shoulders. No long
  elegant neck.
- EARS SET LOW, at eye level, hanging as TWO DISTINCT LOBES that reach about the
  jawline and hang clear of the cheek. Their hair is in longer, looser waves than the
  body, and they are the DARKEST value on her (#6b3f22 against a #8a5230 body). Never
  set high, never short, never the same colour as the body.
- TOPKNOT shapeless and unclipped, held CLEAR OF THE EYES. The eyes are never veiled.
- COAT medium length and UNCLIPPED EVERYWHERE: no shaved face, no shaved feet, no
  pompoms, no bracelets, no rosettes, no clipped edges, no continental or show trim of
  any kind. She is a pet, never a show dog.
- TAIL long and feathered along its whole length. Standing, it is carried UP and
  CURVED OVER THE LINE OF THE BACK. Lying or sitting, it is slack and laid out behind
  her along the ground. Never tucked. The plume is the longer, looser, paler hair of
  the last third (#dda97b), rather than a separate object: it is NOT a fan, NOT a
  feather duster, NOT a squirrel tail, NOT a shaved pom on a stalk, and never wider
  than her own head.
- FEET are cream mops of hair that close over the toes. Pads are dark (#3d2313) and
  appear ONLY when a sole is turned toward the viewer. Never pink pads, never clipped
  feet with bare toes.

=== THE MARKING MAP — cream (#efd3b4) sits in exactly FIVE places and NOWHERE else ===

1. A BAND AROUND THE MUZZLE. It starts just behind the nose and runs back to a line
    under the eyes, and it wraps the whole muzzle including under the jaw. It is a
    BAND, not a mask: it does NOT reach the eyes, it does NOT climb the forehead, it is
    NOT a blaze, and it is NOT a big cream patch covering the front of the face.
2. TWO SMALL CREAM PIPS directly above the eyes, one over each eye, each about the
    size of the eye. ALWAYS drawn — they carry her expression and they are the mark
    most often forgotten.
3. A BIB on the throat and the upper chest only — about the size of a bow tie. NOT a
    shirt front, NOT a belly panel, NOT a stripe running down to the groin.
4. STOCKINGS from about the elbow and the hock down.
5. THE FEET.

The skull, the ear leathers, the back, the flanks, the rump and the legs above the
elbow and hock are BODY COLOUR. There is no cream there at all.

=== THE FIXED FACE (whenever the face is toward the viewer) ===

- Both eyes ROUND, WIDE-SET, OPEN and LEVEL — neither narrowed nor widened — looking
  straight at the viewer. Flat amber iris #c4834e with a distinct flat dark pupil
  #3d2313 inside it. NO white anywhere in the eye: no catchlight, no glint, no
  reflection, no sclera.
- The rim around each eye is a THIN LIVER-BROWN line, never black, never heavier than
  the other interior lines.
- The NOSE is LARGE for the short muzzle, one flat LIVER BROWN #a9663b, NEVER BLACK
  and never with a lighter sheen band across it. Nostrils and the philtrum groove are
  drawn in the white line only. Two dark nostril apertures are allowed; a dark nose
  around them is not.
- Mouth CLOSED at rest: one short flat white stroke, straight, no curve up or down.
  It opens only when the pose explicitly says so, and an open mouth is what a running
  or rolling dog does, never an expression.

=== EXCLUSIONS ===

- She wears NOTHING: no collar, no harness, no tag, no clothes, no hat, no bandana,
  no bow, no booties. She holds nothing. No ball, no toy, no bone.
- Nobody is with her: no second animal, no hand, no person.
- NO LETTERING ANYWHERE: no caption, no label, no title, no annotation, no arrow, no
  measurement, no speech bubble, no watermark, no signature, no logo.
