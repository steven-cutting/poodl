Prompts for the panels of the Biscuit character reference sheet.

Every panel is one call to the generate_image tool that `agy` (Google Antigravity CLI)
exposes, Nano Banana underneath. The prompt is the panel's POSE paragraph below followed by
the whole of style.txt, unchanged. ImagePaths carries at most three files: the canonical
front view (panels/front.jpg) first, then the photographs named, which live under
docs/design/biscuit_pics/raw/cutouts/. Generate each one to three times and keep the best.

Drive it non-interactively with something like:

  agy --print-timeout 7m -p="Use your generate_image tool exactly once, then reply with
  ONLY the absolute path of the generated file. ImageName: <name>. AspectRatio: <ratio>.
  ImagePaths: <files>. Prompt for the tool, pass it verbatim: <POSE> <style.txt>"

The file lands under ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/<conversation>/; copy it to panels/.

CONS, appended to every panel drawn after the canonical front view:
  The first reference image is the approved canonical drawing of this exact character in
  this exact style: match its proportions, its markings, its colours and its line weight
  precisely; the other references are photographs of the real dog.
For the postures CONS continues:
  ... and are evidence for the posture only. Full body in frame, nothing cropped, generous
  margin, no floor line drawn.
