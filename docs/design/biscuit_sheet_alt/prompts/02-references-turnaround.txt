=== REFERENCE IMAGES ===
Images 1 to 7 are photographs of the real dog, cut out of their backgrounds. They are evidence for anatomy, head shape, ear set and length, the placement of the cream markings, the tail plume and the face — nothing else. Their colours are unreliable (faded, overexposed or red-filtered) and must NOT be copied: use the exact palette in the block. Ignore and never draw any collar, harness, tag, grass, sticker border or white outline that appears in them. Image 1 (lying, forelegs forward, looking at the camera) and Image 2 (loafed, looking at the camera) show the adolescent head, the ears and the medium coat length to draw. Image 3 (puppy with both forepaws forward) and Image 4 (puppy from above) show the marking pattern at full contrast, the brow pips and the dark pads — puppy proportions are NOT to be used. Image 5 (flat red profile) shows the true head profile: the short blunt muzzle, the soft stop, the domed skull and the hanging ear — structure only. Image 6 (seen from above and behind, on black) shows the crown, both ear lobes and the pale tail plume from behind. Image 7 (running at the camera) shows the front of the dog and how the ears lift.
