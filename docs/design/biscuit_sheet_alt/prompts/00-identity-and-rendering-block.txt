=== IDENTITY AND RENDERING BLOCK (applies to everything you draw) ===

"Biscuit" is an adolescent brown miniature poodle, the mascot of a small web game. She is a pet poodle, never a show poodle. Draw her as a FLAT VECTOR-STYLE ILLUSTRATION in exactly this rendering language, like a screen-print with one keyline and flat spot colours:

LINE
- One ink line in pure white (#ffffff), the same width everywhere (about 1/150 of the image width). No tapering, no pressure variation, no thicker outer contour, no hatching, no cross-hatching, no cross-contour, no stipple, no sketchy or broken strokes.
- The coat is suggested ONLY by the gentle waver of the silhouette's contour, plus a FEW open loops of the white line inside the silhouette where the hair is longest (the topknot, the ears, the legs, the tail plume) — roughly six to twelve loops in a whole-dog drawing. The interior of every colour area is otherwise EMPTY and FLAT. NEVER a scribble, NEVER an all-over curl texture, NEVER individually drawn fur strands, NEVER a fur pattern.

FILLS
- Flat fills only: every colour area is one completely even colour. NO shading, NO gradient, NO highlight, NO cast shadow, NO ambient occlusion, NO texture, NO paper grain, NO noise. Light does not fall on her.
- Exact palette and nothing else, no mixing, no lighter or darker versions of any of them:
  - body (skull, back, flanks, rump, upper legs): #8a5230 — a medium-dark warm brown
  - ear hair: #6b3f22 — the darkest thing on her
  - cream markings: #efd3b4
  - tail plume: #dda97b
  - nose leather: #a9663b
  - iris: #c4834e (amber)
  - pupil, and paw pads: #3d2313
  - the line: #ffffff
  - background: #0b0b0b (flat near-black, filling the whole image)
- She is recognisably BROWN. The body is NEVER cream, beige, tan, grey, blond or café-au-lait. The reference photographs are faded, overexposed or filtered: take anatomy from them, NEVER colour.

BACKGROUND
- One flat near-black #0b0b0b over the whole image. No floor line, no ground, no horizon, no scene, no props, no vignette, no frame, no border, and NO white halo, glow or sticker outline around her.

ANATOMY
- Square, compact body: height at the withers about equal to the length from chest to rump. Not long-bodied, not leggy.
- Round domed skull with a SHORT BLUNT muzzle and a soft stop: the head is a circle with a stub on the front, never a long tapering wedge. The neck disappears into coat so the head sits close to the shoulders.
- Ears set LOW, at eye level: two distinct drop lobes reaching about the jawline, hanging clear of the cheeks, in longer looser waves than the body.
- A shapeless, unclipped topknot held clear of the eyes (the eyes are never veiled).
- Coat medium length and unclipped everywhere: no shaved face, no shaved feet, no pompoms, no bracelets, no rosettes, no clipped lines, no continental or show trim of any kind.
- Tail carried up in a loose curve, ending in a fuller, paler plume (#dda97b): not a pom on a stalk, not a bare whip.
- Feet are cream mops; the pads are dark (#3d2313) and are visible only when a sole turns toward the viewer. Never pink pads, never exposed toes.

MARKINGS — cream (#efd3b4) sits in exactly FIVE places and nowhere else:
  1. a band around the muzzle, from just behind the nose back to under the eyes;
  2. two small cream pips directly above the eyes (ALWAYS drawn — they carry the expression);
  3. a bib on the chest and throat;
  4. stockings from about the elbow and the hock down;
  5. the feet.
  The skull, the ears, the back, the flanks, the rump and the hind legs above the hock are body colour. No cream there.

THE FIXED FACE (whenever her face is toward the viewer)
- Both eyes round, wide-set, open and level — neither narrowed nor widened — looking straight at the viewer. Amber iris #c4834e with a distinct dark pupil #3d2313. The eye rim is liver brown, NEVER black, and never a heavy dark outline.
- Nose large for the short muzzle, liver brown #a9663b, NEVER black; the nostrils and the philtrum are drawn in the white line only.
- Mouth closed at rest: one short flat white stroke. Deadpan. No smile, no cartoon expression, no raised eyebrow, no tongue unless the pose explicitly says the mouth is open.

EXCLUSIONS
- She wears nothing (no collar, harness, tag, clothing, hat, bandana, bow, booties) and holds nothing. No second animal, no hand, no person, no toy, no ball.
- No lettering of any kind anywhere in the image: no caption, no label, no title, no annotation, no arrow, no speech bubble, no watermark, no signature.
